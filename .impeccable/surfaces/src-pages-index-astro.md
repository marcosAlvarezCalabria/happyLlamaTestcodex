## Scope and mode

One root route with two client-selected modes. Creator is an Operate surface for the seller; Viewer is an Experience surface for the recipient.

## Audience, job, and action

The Happy Llama owner must create an NFC-ready reminder URL quickly on a phone, including while the on-screen keyboard is open. The recipient should understand the personal message immediately and be able to replay the llama's motion without navigating a website.

## Content and constraints

Message, optional signature, and one of eight supplied scenes are the only creator inputs. The viewer is English; the creator is Spanish. Preserve static Astro output, URL-carried content, privacy, 44px targets, and low-end mobile performance.

## Direction and memorable moment

A vivid pocket-retablo reminder joins Peru and Galway. The Higgsfield motion fills the whole screen without a card, the personal message types directly over a contrast-protected upper zone that stays clear of the llama, and the walk plays when tapped.

## Production assets

The supplied scene photographs and transparent llama cutout remain in the creator preview. The viewer uses the Higgsfield tap-triggered walking film as its single edge-to-edge scene.
