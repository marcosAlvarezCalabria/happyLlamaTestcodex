## Scope and mode

One root route with three client-selected modes. Creator is an Operate surface for the seller; Reminder Viewer and Story are Experience surfaces for the recipient.

## Audience, job, and action

The Happy Llama owner must first choose Reminder or How it was made, then create the matching NFC-ready URL quickly on a phone. Reminder recipients understand a personal message immediately; Story recipients see a short, replayable account of Andean textile tradition.

## Content and constraints

Reminder uses message, optional signature, and one of eight supplied scenes. Story has no inputs and always generates `/?v=story`. Recipient modes are English; creator supports Spanish and English. Preserve static Astro output, privacy, 44px targets, and low-end mobile performance.

## Direction and memorable moment

A vivid pocket-retablo reminder joins Peru and Galway. The Higgsfield motion fills the whole screen without a card, the personal message types directly over a contrast-protected upper zone that stays clear of the llama, and the walk plays when tapped.

## Production assets

The supplied scene photographs and transparent llama cutout remain in the reminder preview. The viewer uses the Higgsfield tap-triggered walk; Story uses the three supplied Andean textile photographs and supplied cultural copy without claiming that pictured people made the product.
