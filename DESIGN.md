# Happy Llama Reminder Design

## Visual thesis

The interface behaves like one personal, retablo-inspired reminder rather than a website. It opens directly into the chosen Galway–Peru photograph, the recipient's message, and the llama standing freely inside that scene.

## Colour system

- Happy Pink `#FF0088`: primary action and selected states.
- Ink `#2A2230`: copy and outlines.
- Butter `#FFF5A8`: logo-adjacent highlight and warm surface.
- Sky `#BCEBFF`, Leaf `#4FBF84`, Coral `#FF796A`, Violet `#7456C9`: supporting scene roles.
- Paper `#FFFDF7`: primary background.

Use the full palette in broad scene fields and small folk details. Keep form controls on paper-white surfaces and reserve pink for actions and confirmed selection.

## Type

Nunito Variable is self-hosted through the build. Headings use 800–900; body and controls use 600–750. Copy remains friendly, direct, and short. The personal message and its signature share one display scale: short pairs use approximately twice the standard size, while medium and long pairs step down automatically to preserve the clear zone above the llama.

## Shape and material

The reminder is one full-height Higgsfield motion scene with no inner card or second scenic layer. The personal message sits directly over the upper part of the photograph in large, high-contrast type, with a soft scene-wide contrast fade instead of a container. Creator controls remain sturdy and tactile. Radii are 14–28px; pills are used only for small status labels.

## Composition

Mobile is the primary canvas. The viewer is one edge-to-edge motion background with no framed card, catalogue sections, accessory controls, commercial footer, or duplicate message below the hero. The recipient's message uses a reserved upper text zone that remains clear of the visible llama, including with long copy and short screens. The creator keeps a live preview pinned inside the mobile form; while the keyboard is active it folds into a compact message strip, then returns to the complete preview after typing.

## Motion

One authored moment owns the experience: the message types itself over the scene, then tapping the llama repeats the typing while the Higgsfield walk plays once and returns to its opening frame. A brief fan of stars acknowledges the tap.

## Accessibility and resilience

All interactive targets are at least 44px, focus rings are high contrast, and content is visible without animation. Text over scenes always has a dark contrast layer.
